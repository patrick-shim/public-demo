The company CI guidance requires the symbol and wordmark together. The app icons preserve the complete supplied logo and its aspect ratio. The 192 px color icon adds white padding; the 32 px outline uses the official white variant on transparency. Small outline rendering is necessarily limited by the long wordmark.
